The contents of this thesis is made up of four published papers, each turned into chapters.

These are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Chapter 3 is based on the paper "Riemannian Score-Based Generative Modelling".
    \item Chapter 4 is based on the paper "Diffusion Models for Constrained Domains".
    \item Chapter 5 is based on the paper "Metropolis Sampling for Constrained Diffusion Models".
    \item Chapter 6 is based on the paper "Geometric Neural Diffusion Processes".
\end{itemize}
The next pages declare my personal contributions to these works.
In addition to these contributions I have rewritten large parts of the papers into chapter form.
